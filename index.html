Klera Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026

Klera is software developed for Kaptured Resale to assist with resale operations, including authorised interaction with eBay services. This policy explains how information is handled when Klera is connected to an eBay account.

Information Klera may process

When an eBay user authorises Klera, Klera may process information made available through the eBay APIs within the permissions granted by that user. Depending on the enabled features, this may include seller account information, business-policy information, inventory and listing information, listing status and other information needed to create, manage or verify authorised marketplace operations.

Klera also processes information supplied directly to it for resale work, such as product descriptions, photographs, listing details and related operational records.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide and operate Klera's authorised functionality, including preparing marketplace listings, retrieving seller configuration needed for those listings, carrying out operations explicitly authorised by the user, verifying their outcomes, and maintaining operational and security records.

Klera does not sell personal information or eBay account information.

eBay authorisation

Connection to eBay uses eBay's authorisation process. Klera requests only the permissions required for enabled functionality. Authorisation credentials and tokens are treated as confidential security material and are not intended to be exposed in application logs, source code or public files.

An eBay connection can be disconnected, after which Klera will no longer use the stored authorisation to make new eBay API requests. Users can also manage third-party application permissions through their eBay account where supported by eBay.

Storage and security

Klera is designed as a local-first desktop application. Information required for its operation may be stored locally on the device on which Klera runs. Sensitive authentication material is designed to be stored using protected operating-system credential storage rather than ordinary application files.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information from unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be transmitted to eBay when necessary to perform an eBay function requested or authorised through Klera. Information may also be processed by infrastructure or service providers where this is necessary to provide a Klera feature. Klera does not disclose information to third parties for their independent advertising or data-broker purposes.

Data retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, including operational records, security, dispute handling and applicable legal or accounting obligations. Authentication information that is no longer required should be removed or invalidated as appropriate.

Requests concerning access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with Klera can be made using the contact address below. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate record-keeping obligations.

Third-party services

Klera's use of eBay services is also subject to eBay's own terms and privacy practices. This privacy policy describes Klera's handling of information and does not replace eBay's privacy notice.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Klera develops or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to Klera, contact Kaptured Resale at kapturedresalebargains@gmail.com.
